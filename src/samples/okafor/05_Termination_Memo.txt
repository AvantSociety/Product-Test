RIVERBEND LOGISTICS, INC.
MEMORANDUM — PERSONNEL ACTION

To: Personnel File — Daniel Okafor
From: Laura Chen, Human Resources Manager
Date: March 2, 2026
Re: Termination of employment

Daniel Okafor, Dispatch Supervisor, Montgomery Terminal, was terminated effective March 2, 2026.

Reason for termination: Violation of Employee Handbook section 6 (Attendance). Records show eleven late arrivals between September 1, 2025 and February 27, 2026. Under section 6.3, six or more unexcused late arrivals within a six-month period may result in termination.

No prior written warning was issued. The employee was informed of the decision in a meeting on March 2, 2026 attended by Brent Kowalski, Vice President of Operations, Sharon Willis, Terminal Manager, and the undersigned.

The employee stated that most of the late arrivals were pre-approved by his manager because of his father's medical treatment. Ms. Willis did not dispute this at the meeting. The employee asked whether the decision was related to the DOT inquiry. Mr. Kowalski stated that it was not.

Final pay, including accrued PTO of 6.5 days, will be issued on the next regular payroll date of March 13, 2026. The 2025 performance bonus of 9% of base salary, approved on January 16, 2026, has not been paid and is under review by Operations.

COBRA notice to be mailed within 14 days.

Laura Chen
Human Resources Manager
