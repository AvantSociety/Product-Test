Dear Paul:

This letter provides our initial evaluation following our review of the store's file and the witness statement.

Liability. Our principal concern is notice. Alabama requires the plaintiff to show the store had actual or constructive notice of the hazard. The floor sweep log shows aisle 7 was last inspected at 2:00 p.m., about two hours and forty minutes before the fall, against a store policy of hourly inspections. Mr. Tran's statement that the liquid was visible ten to fifteen minutes before the fall, combined with Ms. Morrow's email reporting prior leaking bottles from the same display that week, gives the plaintiff a strong argument on constructive and possibly actual notice.

Video. The loss of the Camera 7B footage is a serious problem. Ms. Morrow asked for it to be preserved on November 15, 2025, before any claim was made, and it was overwritten on November 28, 2025. We should expect a request for a spoliation instruction. We recommend we disclose the loss promptly rather than have it discovered.

Damages. Medical specials total $9,498.45 billed and $4,805.75 paid. The wrist fracture is documented. The hip claim is weaker given the negative X-ray and the 2023 osteoarthritis history.

Recommendation. Given the notice evidence and the video issue, we recommend authority to explore early mediation in the range discussed on our call, and that we take Mr. Tran's deposition before mediation only if settlement discussions fail.

Please let me know if you would like to discuss.
